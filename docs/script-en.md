# N-V-D-A

### A parody of "Y.M.C.A." — *Eleven Days*

**Format:** English vocal + Chinese hard subtitles (subtitles are not part of this sheet).
**Rule:** every joke lives in the English line. Nothing depends on a footnote.
**Structure:** 51 lines, line-for-line identical to the original — 8 · 8 · 8 · 8 · 8 · 8 · 3.

## Performance notes

1. **The Board Chair never raises his voice.** The joke is the courtesy. "Lose the leather and go" is said the way you'd ask someone to check their coat.
2. **Chorus 1 is cold, not fierce.** "It's hell to be out of the N-V-D-A" is a pleasant little melody with a rotten sentence inside it. Shout it and the joke dies.
3. **Chorus 2 is not a triumph.** Same melody, same faces, same band — only the number changed from down thirty to up thirty-one. Play it exactly like Chorus 1 and let the words do the work.
4. **Verse 2 speeds up.** Trump is enjoying himself; that is the whole character.
5. **Verse 3 is the only section that sounds sincere.** Play it straight. No wink. The turn is in one line — "I said, they never were" — and it lands only if nothing before it was played for laughs.
6. **The last line of Chorus 3 is the thesis.** "As long as the ticker goes up" gets the downbeat. Do not bury it in the fade.

## The three-step ending

The parody turns on one word in the final line of each chorus:

| Chorus | Last line | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | You **can't** do whatever you feel | Not allowed |
| 2 | You **can** do whatever you feel | Allowed |
| 3 | **As long as** the ticker goes up | Conditional |

Verses and choruses are otherwise near-identical by design. The audience sings along twice and hears the condition the third time.

## Meter

Every line matches the original syllable for syllable.

| Section | Original | This version |
|---|---|---|
| Verse 1 | 8 · 10 · 10 · 8 · 8 · 10 · 10 · 8 | same |
| Chorus 1 | 10 · 10 · 11 · 8 · 10 · 10 · 12 · 8 | same |
| Verse 2 | 8 · 10 · 10 · 8 · 8 · 10 · 10 · 8 | same |
| Chorus 2 | 10 · 10 · 11 · 8 · 10 · 10 · 12 · 8 | same |
| Verse 3 | 10 · 10 · 10 · 8 · 8 · 10 · 10 · 8 | same |
| Chorus 3 | 10 · 10 · 11 · 8 · 10 · 10 · 12 · 8 | same |
| Outro | 10 · 10 · 13 | same |

Three deliberate adjustments make this work:

- **"It's hell to be out of N-V-D-A"** drops the article. Ten syllables, exactly matching "It's fun to stay at the Y-M-C-A". English acronyms don't need it.
- **Chorus 1 uses "The ticker is" (11); Chorus 2 uses "The ticker's" (11).** The contraction absorbs the extra syllable in "thirty-one", so both lines land on eleven.
- **"the White House called on me"** replaces "a politician came up to me": eight syllables against twelve, and it names the actual caller.

## Notes on the wordplay

| Line | Double meaning | Subtitle should take |
|---|---|---|
| broke the code | Violated the code of conduct / broke the software (chip company) | The first. Flash a line of error text on screen. |
| on board | Back on the board / back aboard | "Rejoined" |
| board men | Deliberately echoes "boys" and "young man" | "Directors" |

**`cleared` in Chorus 2 is the only outright lie in the song.** On the day of reinstatement — 23 November — the independent committee's investigation was still running, and the board's own announcement said it would continue to conclusion. So Chorus 2 sings the version the market believes, not the facts. Chorus 3's conditional ending is what exposes it. Do not "fix" this.

## Prop: the leather jacket

Three beats, no more:

1. **Verse 1** — the Board Chair takes it off the chair and drops it on the floor.
2. **Outro** — he picks it up. Backlit, low angle.
3. **Last shot** — the zipper. Close, pull, cut to black.

One physical object in the whole video. Everything else is screens.

## Optional voice-over — never in the lyric sheet

If the video needs the investigation stated outright, dub this flatly over the final fade, after the singing ends:

> "The probe is still open. The committee is still meeting. He is on the job and under investigation — at the same time."

It must not go into the lyric text. It adds a line, and a singer or a cover model will try to sing it.
